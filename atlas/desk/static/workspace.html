Guide

Operating Atlas

  1. Brief. Describe the business and the work in the Atlas panel. Plain questions get plain answers and do not create a team. The team is drafted in the Team panel as you talk.
  2. Shape the team. Click a row to read an agent's brief. Say "add a reviewer", "put the writers under one lead", "remove the researcher". Switch to Org chart to drag cards.
  3. Add cameras. Press Add camera, or click a camera tile, and choose an RTSP stream, an uploaded recording, a webcam, a snapshot URL or sample footage. Test shows a frame before you attach it.
  4. Build. Press Build desk. Agents and cameras go live; each camera keeps a written journal.
  5. Give the team a job. New job (or N) opens Runs: every agent's progress, tool calls and output stream into Current run and Activity.
  6. Ask anything. With cameras on, questions such as "Was any bag left unattended?" are answered from the journal with times and frames.
  7. Approve. Outbound messages wait under Approvals: edit, approve or reject with a note.
  8. Keyboard. Ctrl K command palette · / focus the Atlas composer · N new job · Esc close.

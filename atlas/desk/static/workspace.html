Guide
How Atlas works

    
    
      
  1. Talk to Atlas. Say what your business does and what eats your time, in your own words. Tap a suggestion if you want a quick start. The workspace opens on its own as soon as Atlas has a first idea.
    2. 
      
  2. Watch the team appear. Atlas draws the agents it will run for you. Click any card to read its brief. Reshape it by talking: "add a reviewer", "put the writers under one lead", "remove the researcher".
    3. 
      
  3. Add your cameras. Say "I have cameras at the till and the back door" and paste the stream addresses (rtsp://...), or say "try it on sample hotel footage". Each camera keeps a detailed journal of what it sees: who arrives, what they wear and carry, how long they wait, what changes.
    4. 
      
  4. Build. Press Build this desk. The agents and cameras go live. Camera tiles show the latest picture and the latest note.
    5. 
      
  5. Ask anything. Type a question into the chat, for example "Was any bag left unattended?" or "How long did the guest at reception wait?". Atlas answers from the journal, with times, and shows the frames it used.
    6. 
      
  6. Give the team a job. Use the bar at the top: a customer enquiry, a report, a daily summary. Every agent's work streams onto its card as it happens.
    7. 
      
  7. Approve. Nothing leaves without you. Messages the agents want to send wait under Approvals; edit, approve or reject with a note.
    8. 
      
  8. Come back later. Open Atlas again and pick your desk under "Or open one of your desks". The full dashboard is behind the Atlas logo, top left.
